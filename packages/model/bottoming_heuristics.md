

# How to bottom cards

## Land vs. spell

if 5+ lands -> bottom land
if 0-3 lands -> bottom spell
if 4 lands and at least one castable creature or removal (broad definition including counters etc.) for <=3 manavalue -> bottom land
otherwise -> bottom spell

## Which land to bottom
Rules in this order:
1) Keep lands that can make two types of mana over basic lands
2) Bottom lands that are not needed to fulfill color requirements on hand (e.g. if a spell costs 1BB and another 1R, you have 2 mountains and 2 swamps, you bottom a mountain)
3) Bottom a land with the color that is most available
4) Bottom a land whose color has fewer cards in deck

## Which spell to bottom
Rules in this order:
1) Bottom spells that are not yet castable (assuming all lands in hand are in play)
2) Bottom spells whose color requirements are not yet met (assuming all lands in hand are in play)
3) Bottom spell with higher mana value
4) Bottom spell with lower OH WR
